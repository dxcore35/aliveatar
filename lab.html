The lab

Every knob the engine has: drive a state, retune the spring, watch a crowd. None of it is needed to use the avatar — this is the workbench it was built on. How it works is written up in ENGINE.md.

Both are the same person, the same seed and the same animation, driven together — so anything that only works in one theme shows up here immediately.

Turn the microphone on and talk — both avatars will mouth what you say, from the waveform. This is the same path a LiveKit track takes.

Who

Expression

Each button loads two 48-point eye rings. The face springs from where it is to the new shape — it never cuts. The ✦ entries also move the head, so the spring carries that too. Press Test eyes above to watch all forty-one go past in order.

Eye animations

These are not poses — they are performances, with a length and a shape over time. They come from bloub's animated states, where the interesting part is the middle of the move, not where it ends. Each one plays over whatever expression is showing and hands the face back. A person takes a smaller share of the big ones, so the beat is the same on both kinds.

Aim — eyes, head and depth

The Humation face is drawn in three-quarter view, so it starts already turned. Around +27° the eyes reach a straight-on pose; past about ±90° each eye slips behind the head and hides.

Look

Everything here is drawn once and then costs nothing per frame — the texture is a tiled image, not a filter, and the shading is duplicated paths, not an offscreen buffer.

Automation

A blink takes 320 ms: 42 % closing, 58 % opening, never fully flat (4 % floor).

39 states

A state is not one face. It names a pool of expressions, how often to swap between them, how often to blink, and how the body moves.
